          Battlecard: "Our Procore AI does this"

          Updated weekly
          What they'll say
          "Procore's agents already review submittals and draft RFIs, and we already pay for Procore."
          What's true
          Procore launched native agents in May 2026. They're good and they're already installed, so don't argue that point.
          Reframe
          Ask how they're billed. Credit-based pricing means every question costs money and field teams stop asking. Trunk is one flat annual price with unlimited users.
          Prove it
          Offer to run both on one of their real bulletins. 10 to 20% of meaningful revisions aren't clouded by the architect, so text-only review misses them.
          Rebuttal that's winning
          "Run both on your drawings" · 41% stage advance
